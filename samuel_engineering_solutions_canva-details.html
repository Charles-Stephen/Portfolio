Samuel Engineering Solutions - Business Card

Project information

  • Category: Canva Design
  • Project Type: Business Card Design
  • Tool: Canva

Samuel Engineering Solutions is a professional business card design created in Canva for an engineering and manufacturing business. The design presents the company's name, services, contact details, and business information in a clear and professional layout.

The design uses a modern engineering-themed visual style with geometric elements, a technical background, and a structured information layout to create a professional business identity.

Design Features

  • Professional business card layout
  • Engineering and manufacturing themed design
  • Company name and branding
  • Founder and technical lead information
  • Business services and contact information
  • Phone, email, and location details
  • Modern geometric graphic elements
  • Clean and organized typography
